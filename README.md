# 2026-Autumn-Aberdeen-15-Increment

Increment is an English learning app project for university students preparing for class discussions and academic presentations. It helps learners turn English they understand into language they can use independently through a short practice cycle: listen, read, listen again, write, review and revise. The proposed app supports personal audio and video imports, AI-assisted sentence analysis and writing feedback, and optional speaking practice with recording and self-assessment. Completed cycles are recorded automatically to encourage regular practice.
